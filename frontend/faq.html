Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how this app uses AI, what the symptom tools can and cannot do, doctors, data practices, and records systems — in plain language.

The assistant uses AI models to generate conversational guidance, summarize wellness topics, and structure educational outputs (for example, general precautions or types of specialists). It does not examine you, access your medical devices, or replace the judgment of a licensed clinician. Always treat AI output as informational, not authoritative medical advice.

The symptom experience is designed for orientation and education: it may suggest general precautions or categories of doctors to consider. It is not a diagnosis, triage service, or emergency tool. Symptoms overlap across many conditions; only an in-person or telehealth evaluation with appropriate tests can determine what is going on. If you have severe, sudden, or worsening symptoms, seek urgent care or emergency services immediately.

Doctor profiles shown in the app are illustrative samples to help you understand specialties (for example, cardiology or dermatology). They are not live booking integrations with every provider in your area unless your deployment explicitly adds that. AI may suggest a type of doctor to discuss with your primary care team; it does not rank real-world clinicians or guarantee availability, insurance coverage, or quality.

Accounts use secure sign-in (including email verification where configured). Data you save in your profile or chats is stored according to your organization’s hosting and database policies. Use strong passwords, do not share your login, and avoid entering highly sensitive identifiers unless your deployment has been reviewed for compliance (for example HIPAA or GDPR) by qualified professionals. This FAQ cannot replace a formal privacy assessment for your environment.

By default, this application does not automatically read or write your hospital Electronic Medical Record (EMR) or Electronic Health Record (EHR). Information you see here is what you or your deployment store in this app’s own database (profile, notes, appointments you create here, etc.). Integrating with Epic, Cerner, FHIR APIs, or regional health networks requires separate, explicit engineering, legal agreements, and patient consent workflows.

No. The app provides general wellness and educational support only. Do not delay or avoid care because of chat responses. Do not start, stop, or change prescription drugs or dosages based on this tool; those decisions belong with your prescribing clinician.

Diet suggestions are high-level orientation — not a personalized meal prescription from a registered dietitian unless one has reviewed your case. Appointment features depend on how your instance is configured; they may be demos or local scheduling aids and are not a guarantee of clinical capacity at any facility.

Still unsure? Review the medical disclaimer in the site footer and speak with a qualified professional for decisions about your care.
